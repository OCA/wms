This module integrates immediately usable stock visibility into
Shopfloor Reception, helping warehouse operators prioritize urgent
receipts and make informed operational decisions on the fly.

Business Value
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

When the immediately usable quantity drops below zero, every incoming
unit is critical. By surfacing this data directly in Shopfloor during
reception, operators no longer work blindly. They can instantly
recognize high-priority stock and handle it according to your
operational needs.
